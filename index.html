Cerita Bimo

Cerita Bimo is a short animated comedy series about Bimo, a Gen Z who badly wants to succeed. New episodes are shared on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Threads as @bimobesoksukses. All characters and companies in the series are fictional.

Cerita Bimo Studio

Cerita Bimo Studio is the creator's own internal tool. It produces the creator's original episodes and publishes them to the creator's own social media accounts through the platforms' official APIs, and shows the creator their content statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
