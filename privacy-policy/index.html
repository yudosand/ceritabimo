Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Cerita Bimo Studio ("the app") is an internal application operated by the creator of Cerita Bimo (@bimobesoksukses). It is used only by its owner to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Threads, and to show the owner statistics about that content.

Information the app accesses

When the owner connects an account, the platform gives the app an access token, basic profile information (account name and ID) and statistics about the account's own content (for example views and follower counts).

How the information is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to display performance statistics to the owner. The information is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and are only sent to the platforms' official APIs.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect an account in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and can revoke access in each platform's settings. Deletion requests: projectyudos@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

Cerita Bimo Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using the app's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

YouTube data the app accesses: the connected channel's ID and title; its subscriber, view and video counts (YouTube Data API channels.list); YouTube Analytics totals for the channel (views and watch time); and, when the owner publishes, the ID and link of the video the app uploaded (videos.insert). The app does not access other users' data, does not download YouTube videos and does not display YouTube content to anyone.

How it is used: to upload the owner's own episodes to the owner's own channel and to show the owner a progress dashboard. It is not shared with anyone and not used for advertising.

Storage: the OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Channel statistics are refreshed from the API every few hours; statistics older than 30 days are refreshed or deleted, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. The app does not place cookies or other tracking on anyone's device.

Revoking access and deletion: access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Disconnecting YouTube in the app deletes the stored tokens immediately, and all stored YouTube data is deleted within 7 days of revocation or on request to projectyudos@gmail.com.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
