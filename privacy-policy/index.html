Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Cerita Bimo Studio ("the app") is an internal application operated by the creator of Cerita Bimo (@bimobesoksukses). It is used only by its owner to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Threads, and to show the owner statistics about that content.

Information the app accesses

When the owner connects an account, the platform gives the app an access token, basic profile information (account name and ID) and statistics about the account's own content (for example views and follower counts).

How the information is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to display performance statistics to the owner. The information is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and are only sent to the platforms' official APIs.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect an account in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and can revoke access in each platform's settings. Deletion requests: projectyudos@gmail.com.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at Google security settings.
